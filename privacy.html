Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · applies from Tend 1.1

The short version: Without an account, everything you enter in Tend stays on your phone, and we have no copy. AI features are optional. When they're on, only the text a feature needs is sent to Anthropic's Claude, after names and identifiers are removed, and photos are never sent. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you. Accounts, for sharing care with family, are optional and in a TestFlight beta.

We built Tend for caregivers on hard days. This page says exactly where your family's health information goes. US residents should also read our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Using Tend without an account

This is how everyone using Tend from the App Store uses it today. Everything you enter is stored only on your phone, using Apple's on-device storage and its encryption:

  • the care profile and conditions
  • medications, schedules and reminders
  • instructions, including the text of documents you upload
  • symptom logs, wound checks and wound photos
  • your own wellbeing check-ins

Tend keeps backup copies on the phone so an app update can't lose your data. Your phone's own iCloud or computer backup may include Tend's data like any other app's, under Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. We can't recover it, because we never had it.

AI features

AI features read uploaded documents into a care plan, write the morning briefing and caregiver tips, and check symptom entries against your care team's warning signs. They're optional: you choose during setup, and you can turn them off any time in Settings. With AI off, nothing is sent anywhere. Schedules, reminders, logs, the medication list and the appointment summary keep working.

FeatureWhat is sent
Reading instructionsThe document's text
Daily briefingConditions or procedure, day count, number of tasks today, and summaries of active instructions
Symptom checkToday's entry including your notes, a numeric summary of the last 14 days, the care team's warning signs, and instruction summaries
Caregiver tipsConditions or procedure, day count, instruction summaries and warning signs

Before anything is sent, Tend removes identifiers it can recognize:

  • the name of the person you care for
  • email addresses and phone numbers
  • labeled record numbers, such as MRN, NHS, Medicare, patient and insurance IDs
  • Social Security numbers and labeled dates of birth
  • street addresses and UK postcodes

Care team members are described by specialty. Redaction is automatic and can't catch everything printed on a page, so leave out pages you don't want analyzed. Wound photos are never sent.

How it travels. Without an account, requests pass through a small relay we run on Cloudflare, which only forwards them to Anthropic. With an account (beta), they go through our server instead, which first checks that AI is allowed for you and your care circle. Neither stores or logs what you send.

Anthropic processes the text to produce the answer under its commercial terms. It doesn't use it to train models, and it keeps it only for a limited period. See anthropic.com/privacy.

Accounts and shared care circles (beta)

Accounts are in a TestFlight beta and aren't part of the App Store version yet. They're optional, and only needed to share care with other people or to use Tend on more than one device. Signing in sends nothing about the person you care for. Moving a care plan to an account is a separate step that asks for your agreement first.

What we store with an account

  • To sign you in: your email address, or the private relay address Sign in with Apple provides, and an account ID.
  • So your circle knows who did what: the name you choose to show.
  • Care plans you move to your account: the same information listed above, including wound photos. They're visible only to the people in that care circle.
  • Circle details: who is in each circle, their roles, invites (stored only as a scrambled code), and an activity log of who joined and who recorded a dose.
  • Your wellbeing check-ins: visible only to you, never to the circle or its admins.
  • Consent records: what you agreed to, which version of the wording, and when.
  • To run the service: your phone's push notification token, used to tell your circle's phones that something changed (the notification carries no health information), and the times of AI and push requests, kept for two days to prevent abuse.

Where it's stored

On servers run by Supabase in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest. It is not end-to-end encrypted: our server can read it, which it needs to do to sync and apply the access rules. We access it only to run and fix the service and to answer your requests.

Who can see a shared care plan

Access is enforced by our database on every request, not just by the app.

  • Viewers see the care plan and its records.
  • Caregivers can also record doses and tasks, add logs and edit the plan.
  • Admins can also invite and remove people, change settings, and export the circle.
  • The person receiving care, once they join, can see everyone, remove anyone including admins, export everything and close the circle. Nobody can take those rights away.

We don't sell personal data, share it for advertising, or use it to track you across apps or websites. We'll disclose information only if the law requires it, and we'll tell you first unless the law forbids that.

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you delete it or delete the app.
  • With an account: until you delete your account. Deleting it erases your account, name, consents and check-ins at once.
  • Deleted records: they disappear from everyone's app at the next sync, and are erased from the server when the circle is closed.
  • Closing a circle: erases its records at once and its photos within a day.
  • Backups: our hosting provider keeps backups for disaster recovery, which may hold deleted data for up to 30 days.

Your choices and rights

In the app you can:

  • Correct anything by editing it.
  • Delete any record.
  • Export an appointment summary. With an account, you can also export your data, and admins or the person receiving care can export the circle.
  • Turn off AI or usage statistics.
  • With an account, leave or close a circle, or delete your account.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to know what we hold, to get a copy, to correct or delete it, and to appeal if we refuse a request. Email hello@commongenelabs.com with "Privacy request" in the subject. We'll answer within 45 days. If we refuse, reply to appeal and we'll respond within 45 days.

If you're in the EU or UK, we rely on your explicit consent to process health information, given in the app and withdrawable at any time. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Tend is for adults organizing care. A parent or guardian may keep a care plan for their child. Children under 13 must not create accounts, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Security

All traffic is encrypted. On the phone, data is protected by iOS data protection, and wound photos are locked until the phone is unlocked. With an account, our database applies access rules to every request, and our server keys never ship in the app. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our practices change and change the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you've already given us, we'll ask again in the app before it applies to you.
