Common questions

Is Tend really free?

Yes. Tend is free to download and use, with no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Does Tend require an account?

No. Tend works without an account, login or email address: open the app and start. Accounts are only for sharing care with family or using Tend on more than one device, and they're in a TestFlight beta right now (see below).

What kinds of documents can Tend read?

Discharge instructions, clinic letters and medication lists from any doctor, for a surgery recovery or a long-term condition. Upload a PDF or a text file, or paste the text in. Add more documents as new doctors weigh in.

Tend can't read scans or photos of pages yet. If a PDF is a scan, Tend will tell you. Type or paste the instructions instead, or ask the care team for a digital copy, often available in the patient portal.

What if two doctors' instructions disagree?

Tend never merges them on its own. When a new document changes a dose you already have, repeats a medication, or says to stop one, the review screen shows both and asks you to replace, add alongside, or skip. It warns you when a dose changes tenfold or more, which is usually a typo. When in doubt, ask the care team.

Where is my data stored?

Without an account, everything you enter stays on your phone, protected by Apple's on-device storage and encryption, and we have no copy. With a beta account, a care plan you choose to share is also stored on Tend's server in the United States, visible only to the people in your care circle. Full details are in our privacy policy.

What does the AI do, and can I turn it off?

AI features read uploaded documents into a care plan for you to review, write the morning briefing and caregiver tips, and check symptom entries against your care team's warning signs. They're optional: turn them off any time in Settings, and the rest of Tend keeps working.

When AI is on, only the text a feature needs is sent to Anthropic's Claude, after names and identifiers are removed. Anthropic doesn't use it to train models. Photos are never sent.

Does the symptom check diagnose anything?

No. It compares an entry with the warning signs your care team wrote down and with the last two weeks of entries, and tells you plainly whether something matched. It never says everything is "normal". If you're worried, contact the care team, or call your local emergency number in an emergency.

Does Tend work with Apple Health or wearables?

Not yet. Symptoms are entered by hand: pain, fatigue, mood, nausea, temperature and notes. If we add Apple Health, it will be optional and stay on your phone.

How do I share care with my family?

Shared care circles are in a TestFlight beta. Everyone in a circle sees the same plan, a dose given on one phone shows as done on everyone's, and the person receiving care stays in charge. To join the beta, email hello@commongenelabs.com with "Tend beta" in the subject.

How do I delete my data?

Delete any record in the app, or delete the app to remove everything on your phone. With a beta account, you can also delete your account in Settings → Account, which removes it from our server.

Is Tend available on Android or the web?

Not yet. A web app is next, so caregivers in a shared circle can take part without an iPhone, and Android comes after that. If this matters to you, tell us at hello@commongenelabs.com: it helps us prioritize.

Something isn't working. What should I do?

Email hello@commongenelabs.com with what happened, a screenshot if you can, your iPhone model and iOS version, and Tend's version (Settings → About). We'll get back to you as quickly as we can.
