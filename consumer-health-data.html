Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy covers "consumer health data" as defined by Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada's SB 370 and similar state laws. In Tend, that's almost everything you enter about the person you care for, and your own wellbeing check-ins. It adds to our Privacy Policy, which explains how Tend works in full. Where the two differ, the one that protects you more applies.

Categories of consumer health data

Without an account, this information stays on your phone and we don't collect it. If you use AI features, the text described in our Privacy Policy is sent to Anthropic to produce an answer, after names and identifiers are removed. Neither Tend's relay nor our server keeps it.

With an account (beta), when you move a care plan to it or join someone's care circle, we store:

  • Health conditions and treatment: conditions or procedures; care team members by specialty; care instructions, including the text of uploaded documents; medications, doses and schedules; tasks and their completion.
  • Symptoms and observations: symptom logs (pain, fatigue, mood, nausea, temperature, notes), wound checks and wound photos.
  • Caregiver wellbeing: your own sleep, stress, breaks and notes.
  • Identifiers that could link to health data: your email address, display name, circle membership and push notification token.

Why we collect it

Only to provide what you asked for:

  • keep the care plan on your devices and share it with the people you invite
  • send reminders and keep them accurate across phones
  • run the AI features you turned on
  • keep the service secure, for example by limiting request rates

We don't use it for advertising, for profiling unrelated to care, or to train AI models.

Where it comes from

From you, when you enter it or upload a document. From other people in a care circle you belong to, when they enter it. And from the AI features you use, which turn your documents into instructions and tasks for you to review.

Who we share it with

  • People in your care circle, as you direct, when you invite them. Their access depends on their role.
  • Service providers that process it for us, only to run Tend: Supabase (database, sign-in and storage in the United States), Anthropic (AI features, when on), Cloudflare (relay for AI requests without an account), Apple (Sign in with Apple and push delivery; a push carries no health data), and an email delivery provider (sign-in codes; your email address only).
  • When the law requires it, for example a valid court order. We'll tell you unless the law forbids it.

We have no affiliates that receive consumer health data. We do not sell consumer health data.

Consent

We ask for your consent in the app before we collect or share consumer health data beyond what a feature you asked for needs, and we record what you agreed to and when:

  • moving a care plan to your account and sharing it with people you invite
  • joining someone else's care circle
  • AI features
  • usage statistics

You can withdraw consent at any time. Turn AI or usage statistics off in Settings. Leave or close a care circle, or delete your account. Withdrawing doesn't affect what was done before.

Your rights

You can:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and get a list of the third parties it's shared with
  • get a copy of it (in the app, with an account: Settings → Account → Export my data)
  • delete it: delete records, close a circle, or delete your account. Deletion reaches our service providers and, after the backup period in our Privacy Policy, our backups.
  • withdraw consent, as above

Email hello@commongenelabs.com with "Health data request" in the subject. We verify the request with your account's email and answer within 45 days. That can be extended once by 45 days when reasonably necessary, and we'll tell you why. If we decline, you can appeal by replying to our decision, and we'll answer within 45 days. If you're still not satisfied, you can contact your state attorney general, for example the Washington State Attorney General.

We don't discriminate against you for using these rights.

Geofencing

We don't use geofencing or location data.
